⚙️ How DecisionLens Works

1. Data Input

Business CSV or Excel data is uploaded into DecisionLens AI.

2. KPI Calculation

DecisionLens automatically detects relevant business columns and calculates meaningful KPIs.

3. Driver Analysis

The system identifies important contributors such as volume, inventory, price, marketing and stockout risk.

4. AI Narrative

Analytical results are converted into business-friendly insights, evidence and recommendations.

5. Confidence

Each insight includes a confidence score to communicate analytical reliability.
